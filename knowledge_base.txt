
TongueTie learning principles

Vocabulary: Teach meaning in context, part of speech, pronunciation, examples, related words and spaced review.

Grammar: Explain rules simply, show affirmative/negative/question forms, then provide controlled and free practice.

Speaking: A production system should transcribe speech, compare it with the expected phrase, identify pronunciation/fluency issues, and provide actionable feedback.

Translation: Preserve meaning, register, cultural context and ambiguity. For languages with multiple scripts or dialects, explain the selected variety.

Pronunciation: Use the target language's own phonology. Do not invent an audio result. Pronunciation scores require an actual speech assessment service.

Safety: Language learning is educational. Do not treat translation or AI explanations as professional legal, medical or financial advice.
